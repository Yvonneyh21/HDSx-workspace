
# Figure Caption

This figure shows the unweighted mean BMI (kg/m²) across NHANES cycles for adults aged 20–80, grouped by income level. The data come from the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset.

Records with missing BMI, Age, IncomeGroup, or Cycle values were excluded. Groups with fewer than 30 observations were also removed.

The BMI axis is zoomed in to make small differences easier to see, which may exaggerate the visual differences between groups. This analysis is descriptive and unweighted, so the results cannot establish causation or be considered nationally representative.
